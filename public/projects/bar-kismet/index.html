Bar Kismet

Draft case study — copy prepared for review.

A visual identity for Bar Kismet, a restaurant centred on fresh seafood and handmade pasta. The work pairs an expressive wordmark with a restrained colour palette across menus, signage and printed materials.

Challenges

Translate the character of the restaurant into an identity that works both at the table and on the street. The design needed to remain recognisable across intimate printed details and exterior signage.

Outcome

The resulting identity is shown across business cards, menus, stationery and storefront applications. Typography, colour and illustration create a consistent visual thread throughout the restaurant experience.
