Starling Architects

Draft case study — copy prepared for review.

A visual identity for Starling Architects, built around the idea of architecture as a living dialogue. The work translates that direction into a wordmark, symbol and collection of branded applications.

Challenges

Express an architectural practice through a visual language that balances precision with character. The identity needed to work across stationery, presentation materials and signage at a range of scales.

Outcome

The identity is shown across stationery, business cards, site signage and architectural hoarding. Its symbol, typography and muted palette create a consistent presence across the collection.
