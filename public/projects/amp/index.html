Advanced medical pods.

Canadian Space Agency

Draft case study — copy prepared for review.

Advanced Medical Pods explores a connected healthcare experience across physical spaces and digital interfaces. The work includes identity, interface concepts and visual applications for medical pods.

Challenges

Create a visual language that can connect a healthcare service, its digital touchpoints and its physical environment. The interface and identity needed to read clearly across different screen sizes and applications.

Outcome

The work brings together a distinctive identity, pod interface concepts and remote consultation screens. Supporting applications show how the system can carry through to the physical pod environment.
