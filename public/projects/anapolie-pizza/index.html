Anapolie Pizza

Draft case study — copy prepared for review.

A visual identity for Anapolie Pizza, celebrating the character of a neighbourhood pizza brand. The work centres on a distinctive wordmark and supporting identity elements across packaging and signage.

Challenges

Bring the brand’s personality into everyday customer touchpoints. The visual system needed to work on pizza packaging, exterior signage and supporting promotional applications.

Outcome

The work brings together an expressive wordmark, character illustration and warm colour palette. Packaging and signage applications show how the identity carries through the customer experience.
