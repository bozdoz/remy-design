Collab.

Draft case study — copy prepared for review.

Collab brings shared memories together through a group photo and video experience. The work explores how a welcoming identity and approachable interface can connect guests, events and the moments they capture.

Challenges

Explain a shared capture experience clearly, from joining an event to contributing a memory. The identity needed to feel at home across the mobile interface, event signage and promotional material.

Outcome

The design brings the Collab identity into mobile screens, event invitations and campaign applications. A consistent visual language connects the product experience with the moments and communities it serves.
