Beaver Creek Vineyard

Draft case study — copy prepared for review.

A visual identity for Beaver Creek Vineyard, expressed through wine labels, packaging and branded materials. The work brings a warm, considered character to the brand across bottle, can and print applications.

Challenges

Bring a consistent visual language to a range of packaging formats while retaining the vineyard’s individual character. The design needed to work at the scale of a bottle label as well as larger brand applications.

Outcome

The resulting collection brings together typography, illustration, colour and packaging into a coherent brand expression. The work shown includes wine labels, canned products and supporting identity applications.
