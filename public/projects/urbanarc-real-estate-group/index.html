Urbanarc Real Estate Group

Draft case study — copy prepared for review.

A visual identity for Urbanarc Real Estate Group, applied across property marketing and brand materials. The work combines an architectural symbol with clear typography and a warm, restrained palette.

Challenges

Create an identity that remains legible and recognisable across property advertising, printed materials and building signage. Each application needed to connect back to the same brand language.

Outcome

The resulting system is shown across billboard advertising, stationery, storefront signage and an open-house promotion. The identity provides a consistent foundation for these different marketing formats.
